# CampusCart

> A simple, well structured CLI-based stock, cart and receipt management solution built for campus vendors to aid seamless business activities

## OVERVIEW

CampusCart is a Command-Line Interface (CLI) application designed to help campus-based vendors manage their dialy sales and inventories more efficiently.

Among many others, this will allow vendors to:
- Track available stock
- Add products to a customer's cart
- Calculate cart totals
- Generate Receipts
- Manage basic product information. For example, name, discount, etc.

---

## PROBLEM STATEMENT
Often times, campus vendors and student businesses rely on manual methods such as notebooks, calculators, basic spreadsheets to manage their inventory and sales.

These methods can make it difficult to:
- Keep accurate records of available stock
- Calculate customer totals quickly
- Track products consistently
- Generate proper receipts
- Reduce human calculation errors

Hence, the CampusCart aims to provide a simple and accessible solution to these problems through a lightweight commnd-line application.

## TARGET AUDIENCE
CampusCart is primarily designed for:
1. **Student Businesses**: Students running small businesses within or around campus.
2. **Pop-up Vendors**: Vendors who operate temporarily at campus events or location.
3. **Small Campus Shops**: Small-scale vendors who need a simple way to manage stock and sales.

---

## MAJOR VALUE PROPOSITION
CampusCart provides a simple way for campus vendors to manage their day-to-day sales operations without requiring complex businesss software.
